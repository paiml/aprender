<objective>
Implement the production loader half of `setfit-apr-v1`: a fail-closed validation ladder ending in
probe replay and the `VerifiedSetFitModel` typestate — the ONLY door to classification — plus the
bounded reader every filesystem caller must use, the induced-corruption negative suite, and the
out-of-crate trybuild non-constructibility proof. Rungs 1-5 are additionally exposed as the
parse-only door `read_setfit_apr_parts` (04-05's codec dependency — landed here so wave 4 stays
conflict-free).

Purpose: APR-02 (fail before prediction), APR-04's consumer side (nothing short of a verified model
reaches any consumer — structurally, because the classify-capable type cannot exist otherwise), and
APR-05's data recovery (everything inspect needs, recovered here once).

**Review findings closed here:** B5 — the 256 MiB cap previously ran only AFTER a caller had already
done `fs::read` on a hostile file; `read_setfit_apr_bytes_bounded` moves the bound in front of the
allocation and every CLI/serve adapter is required to use it. B1 — the head tensors join the
expected tensor set, so a headless artifact cannot load. The LOW finding about a 256 MiB unit-test
allocation is closed by the limit-injected internal helper.

Output: `read_setfit_apr_bytes_bounded` + `load_setfit_apr` + `read_setfit_apr_parts` +
`VerifiedSetFitModel` (incl. `embed`) + negatives + trybuild case.

Branch: `gsd/phase-4-apr-parity` (check exists, stop if not). Wave 3, sole plan in the wave.
</objective>
