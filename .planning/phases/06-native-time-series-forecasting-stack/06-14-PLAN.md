### The reachable range of C-06, for the bound's derivation

`lambda = changepoint_count(n) * (t_max - 1)` with `t_max = (fut.last - ds.first) / (ds.last - ds.first)`.
`changepoint_count` is at most 25 (`Spec::default_linear`) and needs `floor(n * 0.8) - 1 >= 25`, i.e.
`n >= 33` points. The tightest legal span for 33 daily points is 32 days; the widest legal future span
is `3650` `MS` steps ~= 111 000 days. So the STRUCTURAL MAXIMUM is
`25 * (111000 / 32 - 1)` ~= **86 700**, which is exactly the review's measured configuration. Every
larger-history request has a proportionally smaller `t_max`, so high-lambda requests are necessarily
short-history requests — which is why `MAX_POINTS`, `MAX_SPAN_DAYS` and `MAX_HORIZON` are each
satisfied and their product is still unbounded, the same shape as `MAX_HOLIDAY_DESIGN_COST`.

Interpolating the review's four measured points (lambda ~2 825 -> 0.231 s, ~2 866 -> 0.209 s,
~19 925 -> 0.634 s, ~86 700 -> 2.334 s) gives ~2.5e-5 s per unit of lambda over a ~0.15 s floor, so
the 2 s bar sits near lambda ~74 000. **20 000 is the proposed value** — the largest round value with
roughly 3x measured headroom (0.634 s at ~19 925). Task 1 MEASURES it at three compositions and lowers
it if any composition fails to clear 2 s.
