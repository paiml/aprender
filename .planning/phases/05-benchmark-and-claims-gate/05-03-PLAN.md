<objective>
Decide, on measured evidence, what replaces the 10x/10x epsilon window rule at production step
count — and land the F-10 unblock only under a rule that survives.

The previous version of this plan froze "fresh per-class epsilon per the 10x/10x rule" as a second
regime entry. Plan 05-01 was front-loaded precisely so that premise would fail early if it was
going to (D-01). It did: five of six parameter classes have NO legal epsilon at the production
envelope. So this plan can no longer freeze epsilon under that rule, and the replacement is a
decision with a blocking human gate, not a number an executor picks.

What did NOT fail: separation. `ctrl_max = 0.000e0 < real_min` for all six classes in every
measured cell — the 1e-30 control writes back bit-identical weights even at 1536 steps. Real
training remains cleanly distinguishable from no training. This plan must not be executed as
though calibration were impossible; what failed is epsilon-freezing under one specific rule.

Branch: `gsd/phase-2-contract-gate` (02-01 policy; no PR — human's call).

Purpose: until an approved production regime lands, no user-reachable path produces a
`setfit-apr-v1` and every downstream benchmark plan stays blocked. This is still the phase's
keystone; it is now a keystone with a decision inside it.
Output: the decision memo, and — if and only if the human approves a basis — one commit touching
contract + constants + tests together, with pv validate/diff evidence and 05-14's fail-closed
derivation turning GREEN for a recorded reason.
</objective>

<evidence_that_forced_this_revision>
Read `05-CONTEXT.md` § "Amendment — Execution Evidence (2026-08-17, from plan 05-01)" for the
authoritative statement. The load-bearing facts, so this plan can be executed without
re-deriving them:

**The rule.** The contract's DERIVATION invariant requires
`10 * worst_control_delta <= eps <= best_real_delta / 10` across ALL measured cells, with the upper
edge rounded DOWN to two significant figures. `worst_control_delta` is instantiated by two
conditions: the 1e-30 null control and the 1e-8 near-null control.

**What collapsed, and why it is the near-null leg and not the control.** The 1e-30 control writes
back bit-identical weights at 1536 steps (101 rows, max `relative_delta` 0.0, zero rows moved),
so `worst_ctrl` is pinned at exactly `0.000e0` and the near-null leg alone sets every lower bound.
Near-null grew 56–72x from 24 to 1536 steps for five classes and 299x for `attention_key_bias`.
The four-cell basis (`s8:13, s8:31, s8:53, s64:13`, 12 passes):

| class | lower `10 x worst_nnull` | upper `best_real / 10` | window | lower exceeds upper by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `embedding` | 1.553e-3 | 1.813e-4 | **EMPTY** | 8.56x |
| `layer_norm_weight` | 4.802e-6 | 1.891e-5 | EXISTS | — |
| `layer_norm_bias` | 9.844e-4 | 7.112e-5 | **EMPTY** | 13.84x |
| `projection_weight` | 1.051e-3 | 1.231e-4 | **EMPTY** | 8.54x |
| `projection_bias` | 1.101e-3 | 3.447e-5 | **EMPTY** | 31.94x |
| `attention_key_bias` | 9.278e-8 | 1.714e-8 | **EMPTY** | 5.41x |

Cross-cell mixing makes it worse, but the collapse is real within s64 alone
(`3.683e-7 / 9.278e-9 = 39.7`, against a rule needing `> 100`).

**The basis is PROVISIONAL.** 3 of 6 boundary cells measured for the s64 half — `s64:31` and
`s64:53` were never run. Unmeasured cells can only NARROW windows further: within a fixed rule the
lower bound is monotone non-decreasing and the upper monotone non-increasing in measured cells.

**What is NOT in question.** Separation. Also: debug and release measure bit-identically (the build
profile is a price, not a degree of freedom); persisted evidence is bit-identical across processes;
and the architecture component `minilm-slice-h384-l6-a12-i1536-v30522@1110a243` is byte-identical
across `cells=s8e1b16` and `cells=s64e1b16` over a genuinely cross-half 12-pass combine — which is
what makes the collapse a result about STEP COUNT rather than about two different models.

**Explicitly untested.** The superlinear-growth *hypothesis* (299x over a 64x step increase
suggesting the s8 near-null is underflow-dominated). It must not be stated as a mechanism, and
nothing in this plan depends on it.
