PREFILL-CPU (#2787) — batched CPU prefill, raw measurement data.

NOT a §4.4-conformant perf-gate receipt and not a cell measurement. This is a
before/after on ONE host for ONE change, taken with a purpose-built harness
(crates/aprender-serve/examples/perf_cpu_prefill_repro.rs), not with
scripts/perf_gate.sh. It does not move any cell in scripts/perf-matrix.yaml,
which was not modified.

HOST      lambda / noah-Lambda-Vector, AMD Ryzen Threadripper 7960X, 48 threads,
          x86_64 with avx2+fma+avx512f+avx512_vnni. The box was under other
          load throughout: 1-min load average went 13.1 -> 50.0 across the
          interleaved run (and per-replicate load is stamped in the .stderr.txt),
          which is why the arms are interleaved and why every replicate is
          printed rather than a median. One batched replicate (rep 2) shows a
          decode collapse to 2.856 tok/s from that load; its PREFILL was still
          40.566 tok/s. Nothing is dropped.
MODEL     /home/noah/models/qwen2.5-coder-7b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf
          (the W1 model; sha256 509287f78cb4... per evidence/perf-054)
SHAPE     513-token prompt, 128 generated, greedy (temperature 0), c=1.
          W1's shape; NOT W1's prompt corpus — the prompt here is a fixed
          repeated English sentence tokenized by the model's own vocabulary,
          truncated to exactly 513 tokens.
