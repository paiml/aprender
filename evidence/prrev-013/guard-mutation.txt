WHAT THE NUMBER DOES AND DOES NOT COVER
---------------------------------------
The set is DERIVED, not listed — one `drop` and one `flip` per `reject B<n>` site,
rescanned from scripts/check_pr_review_receipt.sh on every run, plus named single-line
edits to the control machinery that must each match exactly one line or the sweep
refuses to run. So 185 is a property of the guard as it stands, and it moves when the
guard does. That is why scripts/check_pr_review_counts.sh derives it rather than
reading it: four files stated "119/119" over a set that had grown to 185, and each of
those four was true when written.

It covers scripts/check_pr_review_receipt.sh. It does NOT cover the two scripts
PRREV-013 adds — check_pr_review_arm4.sh and check_pr_review_counts.sh — which carry
their own `--self-test` case tables, both polarities, wired ahead of the checks they
guard in ci.yml's `pr-review-receipt` job.
