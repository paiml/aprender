    * ONT-2a: L4 is what the tree grounds, not what the contract claims (Refs PMAT-3222)
    
    `is_lean_proved` took a contract's own `verification_summary` as authoritative whenever it claimed

    asserting its own proof was therefore the evidence for that proof. ONT-001 R-4 (andon before mechanism)
    says to withdraw that credit now, in the open, and let ONT-3b earn it back with real grounding.
    
    WHAT GROUNDS A CLAIM is a sorry-free in-tree Lean theorem an equation names and `lean_theorem_names()`
    resolves. That is now the only thing granting L4/L5. A claim with nothing under it is SELF-DECLARED:
    flagged per contract, counted in the totals, printed in its own `L4 evidence` column as `self-declared`
    beside `grounded`, and excluded from L4. `not_applicable` credit still comes from the summary — it is a
    claim about APPLICABILITY, not about a proof, and ONT-8's evidence block is where it gets its provenance.
