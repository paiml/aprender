## Why this is a lower-bound friendly derivation

`wall_seconds` in `smoke-metadata-2026-04-22.json` is the per-epoch aggregate (one
epoch = 25 steps), not a per-step series. The mean over 25 steps includes:

- Pre-warm amortized over 25 steps (one-time cost, typically 200-400 ms).
- Early-epoch GPU ramp (first 2-3 steps slower than steady-state).
- Validation pass (the smoke does 1 val per epoch; val is forward-only so
  cheaper than a training step).

A per-step JSONL emission would give the tighter median-over-steps[20..49]
measurement the contract prescribes. Even with a worst-case 300 ms pre-warm
amortization, steady-state per-step is ≥ (1453 - 300) / 22 = 52.4 ms. Still
9.5× under budget.

## Gap blocking ACTIVE_WITH_LIVE_EVIDENCE

`apr pretrain --json` currently emits one summary object per epoch. To
promote GATE-004 from PARTIAL_ALGORITHM_LEVEL → ACTIVE_WITH_LIVE_EVIDENCE
the CLI needs per-step `{"step": k, "wall_ms": t}` JSONL, then a 50-step
dispatch with median over `step ∈ [20, 49]` computed from the emitted series.

The algorithm-level signal — that a real-compute GPU smoke produced
25 steps in 1.45 s with PID-bound 5492 MiB residency — is already
sufficient to refute the failure mode the gate exists to prevent
(GPU path too slow / silent CPU fallback). The JSONL follow-up is
precision, not correctness.
