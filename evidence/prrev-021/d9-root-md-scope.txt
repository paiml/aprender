=== 3. THE ANCHOR IS THE RULE ===

`*.md` alone matches `docs/x.md` and `evidence/**/results.md`: bash case globs do not
stop at `/`. That would re-admit the docs/ surface PRREV-012 deliberately MEASURED OUT
at 2/5 = 40% precision, below S7's >=90% admission bar. `*/*) : ;;` consumes every path
carrying a separator. Three committed rows hold it:

  MATCH     README.md                    the scar
  MATCH     CHANGELOG.md                 the row that used to record the gap
  NO-MATCH  crates/apr-cli/CHANGELOG.md  single-variable control: same filename, nested
  NO-MATCH  docs/README.md               the anchor's own control: same filename, docs/

In check_no_claim_literals.sh the same anchor is the `:(glob)` pathspec magic. A bare
'*.md' matches 3460 files there against 6.

  $ git ls-files ':(glob)*.md' | wc -l   ->  6
  $ git ls-files '*.md'        | wc -l   ->  3460
