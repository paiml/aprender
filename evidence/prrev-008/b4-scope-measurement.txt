PRREV-008 — B4's diff scan, scoped by measurement rather than by argument.

THE QUESTION. B4 now recomputes comparative claims from the diff (F1). Over WHICH added
lines? S3.C.1 says "in the diff, PR body, docs, or benchmark output". The first draft
took that literally: every added line of every shipped .rs and every .md under book/ and
docs/.

THE MEASUREMENT. That scan was run over the last 300 commits of origin/main. It fires
five times, on two commits, both of them APR-PERF-GATE-001:

  A    d1b7d1995  crates/apr-cli/src/dispatch.rs                             // 15.7 tok/s decode, 0.099x llama.cpp — and it was the surf
  A    ce712eae0  crates/apr-cli/src/commands/serve/server.rs                // #2696: this printed "Performance: 800+ tok/s (2.8x Ollama
  AB   ce712eae0  docs/benchmarking-gate-spec.md                 | 2 | **apr does not batch.** Aggregate throughput is flat at ~110 tok/s
  AB   ce712eae0  docs/benchmarking-gate-spec.md                 | 4 | **The book publishes a number no harness produced** — "851.8 tok/s
  AB   ce712eae0  docs/benchmarking-gate-spec.md                 ### 5.2 Failure #4 — 2.93× Ollama from a harness that never ran Ollama

  (variant A = the .rs half only; AB = also reached by the #2763-faithful line test.)

THE VERDICT. TWO of the five are real comparative claims a comparator could be recorded
for -- `0.097x llama.cpp at c=16` in a docs table with a provenance column, and
`0.099x llama.cpp` in a code comment. THREE quote a fabricated claim IN ORDER TO BAN IT:

  "The book publishes a number no harness produced -- '851.8 tok/s = 2.93x Ollama'"
  "5.2 Failure #4 -- 2.93x Ollama from a harness that never ran Ollama"
  "// #2696: this printed 'Performance: 800+ tok/s (2.8x Ollama)'"

Those three have NO HONEST REMEDY. S3.C.1's exit is a recorded comparator command,
version, env hash, artifact hash and log path. There is no comparator log for a number
nobody measured, so the only way to satisfy the gate is to invent one. A blocking class
whose exit is to fabricate the evidence it demands is worse than the hole it closes.

S7 already decides this without needing the argument: "a class may block only while its
measured precision on the rolling sample is >=90%". 2/5 is 40%.

THE SCOPE THAT SHIPPED. book/**.md, plus PRINTED literals and doc comments in shipped
.rs -- the split check_no_claim_literals.sh already measured (it applies its own RATIO_RE
to print macros and to ///|//! and to nothing else in .rs), and the surface where
"2.93x Ollama" was actually published to readers.

Over the same 300 commits that scope fires ZERO times. Stated as what it is: no measured
false positives AND no measured true positives. It is not evidence of precision. It is
evidence that this repository has not published a competitor ratio to the book in 300
commits, which is the only thing 0/0 can mean.

WHAT IT STILL CATCHES, and why the scope is not a retreat: fixture rows 16 and 22 are
both RED under it, and row 16 is the E1 probe from the PRREV-007 backtest promoted to a
committed fixture -- a head adding `apr sustains 2.93x Ollama on 1.5B Q4_K decode.` to
book/, which the guard at 1e09ca749 ACCEPTED. Row 22 carries the same ratio twice in one
.rs file and the guard must fire on the format! and not on the comment two lines above.

RESIDUAL, RECORDED RATHER THAN HIDDEN. A comparative claim added to docs/ prose or to a
plain // comment is not blocked by B4. The two real instances found in 300 commits are
named above. Closing that residual needs a way to say "this line quotes a claim rather
than making one", which is a receipt-schema change and not this ticket.
