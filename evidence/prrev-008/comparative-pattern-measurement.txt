PRREV-008 F5 — the comparative pattern, measured against the corpus rather than against

WHAT WAS WRONG. B4's COMPARATIVE_RE was a SECOND implementation of a rule this repository
already had: scripts/check_no_claim_literals.sh's RATIO_RE, which #2763/PERF-049 had
already hardened twice. The second copy allowed a ZERO-word gap between the ratio and the
competitor (a closed connector list: faster than / vs / over / of) where #2763 had MEASURED
a five-word bound. So `36.9x over FasterTransformer` — the spelling APR-PERF-GATE-001 §0.1
uses for its [X] figures, and the literal #2763 hardened its own guard to catch — did not
match.

The 13-row case table passed 13/13 over that hole, because its subjects were written from
COMPARATIVE_RE's own vocabulary. A guard universe built from the wrong side.

THE FIX is not a wider pattern of its own: it is #2763's RATIO_LEFT_RE / MULT_RE /
RATIO_GAP_RE composition with the two competitor lists unioned, plus one deliberate
widening (`[[:space:]]*` before the multiplication sign, so `2.93 × Ollama` matches — a
MUST-MATCH row of the backtest corpus that #2763's own pattern misses).
